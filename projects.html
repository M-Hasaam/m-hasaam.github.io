Embedded AI Paperball Collector

On-Device Paper-Ball Detection for an ESP32 Robot

An AI-based embedded robot project: a YOLO11n detector that finds crumpled paper balls on the floor and runs live on a phone or inside a web browser, built for an ESP32-CAM / ESP32-S3 car with a servo arm.

Trained and evaluated end to end: a cleaned dataset with hard negatives, an iterated detector (v1 to v2.1) and a live browser demo that runs fully on the device. The model is exported to TFLite for an Android app and to ONNX for the browser.
